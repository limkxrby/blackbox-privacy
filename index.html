Blackbox Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy covers the Blackbox Chrome extension.

What Blackbox stores

  • Blackbox stores tab URLs, titles, and group names only on the device.
  • The device key stays in chrome.storage.local.
  • A passphrase backup is ciphertext and is not uploaded.
  • There is no account, no analytics, and no server.

How the data is used

  • The data is used only for session restore.
  • The data is not sold.
  • The data is not used for advertising.
  • The data is not transmitted off the device.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/limkxrby/blackbox-privacy/issues.
